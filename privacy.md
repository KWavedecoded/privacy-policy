# Privacy Policy

Tab Volume Mixer respects your privacy.

## Information We Process

Tab Volume Mixer processes audio from the currently selected Chrome tab to provide per-tab volume control.

The extension processes this audio locally on your device. Tab audio is not recorded, stored, uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties.

The extension does not operate a remote server for audio processing.

## Local Settings

The extension may store its volume setting locally on your device using Chrome's local extension storage.

This local setting is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

## Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell user data.

We do not transmit user data to third parties.

We do not use user data for creditworthiness or lending purposes.
